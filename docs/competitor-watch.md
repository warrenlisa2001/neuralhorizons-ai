# Competitor watch

Updated weekly. Purpose: keep Neural Horizons AI's positioning, pages and search visibility ahead of the market.

## Who we track

| Group | Competitors | What to watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close and reconciliation software | BlackLine, Trintech, FloQast, HighRadius, Kognitos | Headline claims, new AI features, agentic wording, case studies, pricing |
| Revenue assurance and leakage | Uniphore, OneBill, Taxilla, Trullion, Xfactrs | Positioning on leakage, order-to-cash claims, new modules |
| Agentic AI platforms and governance | Beam AI, Dataiku, Airia, Moxo | Human-in-the-loop and governance language, GCC partnerships |
| GCC AI consultancies and integrators | PwC Middle East, Bell Integration, Appinventiv, Jacky's Business Solutions, CNTXT AI, RAQMEA | New agentic offers, sector pages, local partnerships, thought leadership |
| Directories and analysts | Clutch (UAE AI), G2 reconciliation category, IDC MarketScape GCC | Who is listed and ranked, category changes |

## Each week, check

1. Their homepage, product and sector pages: new headlines, claims, proof points and offers.
2. Their blog and press: anything on agentic AI, reconciliation, leakage, AI governance in the UAE and GCC.
3. Search: who ranks for "AI revenue leakage detection", "agentic AI consulting UAE", "AI governance GCC", "AI for retail UAE", "AI for IT spend", and whether Neural Horizons AI is named in AI answers for them.
4. New entrants and new GCC partnerships or funding.

## Output

A short report: what changed, what it means for our positioning, and up to three recommended site or content changes. Anything we should copy, counter or ignore, with the reason.

- Verified first: every finding traces to a source row.
- Departments, not one team: the whole trail from order to invoice, briefed to each owner.
- Human in the loop on every action that matters.
- Deployed with you by a founder-led team in the UAE and GCC.
