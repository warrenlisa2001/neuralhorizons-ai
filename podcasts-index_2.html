In the Meantime — 5 AI Podcasts Worth Your Time

1Lex Fridman Podcast

Hosted by Lex Fridman

The biggest AI podcast on YouTube by a wide margin — long-form, deeply researched conversations with the researchers, founders and scientists building frontier AI.

4.9M+ subscribers 850M+ views

2Dwarkesh Podcast

Hosted by Dwarkesh Patel

Deeply researched, hours-long interviews with the people setting AI lab strategy — Anthropic's Dario Amodei and Nvidia's Jensen Huang among recent guests. Widely credited with defining how the industry now talks about AI progress.

Individual episodes routinely pass 1M+ views

3No Priors

Hosted by Sarah Guo & Elad Gil

A venture-capital lens on AI — two of the sector's most connected investors talk to founders and researchers about where the technology and the money are actually heading.

Weekly episodes

4Hard Fork

Hosted by Kevin Roose & Casey Newton, The New York Times

The New York Times' flagship tech show, now with a fast-growing YouTube presence — a plain-English, mainstream-friendly read on the week's biggest AI stories.

Weekly, NYT-backed

5AI at a16z

Andreessen Horowitz

One of the best-funded, best-produced shows in the space — a16z's partners and portfolio founders unpack what's actually shipping in AI, backed by a firm with a front-row seat to it.

Weekly, VC-backed
